# Source: "5 Things Your (Atlanta) Wedding Planner Can Do For You" (Google Doc 11azxL…, owner edeneraweddings@gmail.com)
Usable advice (location removed):
1. Budgeting and staying within it: a planner helps you set a budget AND stick to it; steers you to options that fit; "starting your marriage on solid financial ground".
2. Vendor coordination and contracts: when to book vendors, what questions to ask, reviewing contracts thoroughly, instead of hours of Googling.
3. Design and styling: turns a Pinterest board into a cohesive design that reflects the couple's personality.
4. RSVPs and details: tracking RSVPs and last-minute details, especially when balancing work and planning.
5. Day-of coordination: runs the timeline, manages vendors, handles hiccups "without you ever knowing" so you can be present.
Notes: no statistics in source. Opening claim: hiring a planner "even if you're a DIY enthusiast".
