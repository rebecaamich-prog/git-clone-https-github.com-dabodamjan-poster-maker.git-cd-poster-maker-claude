# Eden Era Events — Brand Voice (interview notes, in progress)

## 1. Ideal client
- Couples in their 20s; both working professionals with full plates.
- Want a planner to lead and guide them — they rely on Rebeca's direction.
- Typical engagement: **partial planning**, then a smooth, seamless wedding day.
- Main stress: overwhelm. Planning tasks piling on top of demanding jobs; they stress easily when decisions stack up.
- What Rebeca gives them: guidance, calm, de-stressing the details, a wedding day that runs smoothly.
- Reference couple: a past partial-planning couple (names kept private). Do not name or quote real clients without written permission.

## 2. What they fear
1. **Budget**: overspending, surprise costs.
2. **Being stuck fixing problems** on the wedding day instead of enjoying it.
3. **Decision overwhelm**: too many choices on top of busy careers.
4. **Not being present** at their own wedding.

Content angle: every education post should relieve one of these four fears. Fear #4 is the emotional core and fits the "Guest of Honor" kit naming (you're the guest of honor, not the crew).

## 3. What Rebeca wants to be known for
- **Calm**
- **Organized**
- **Detail-obsessed**
- **Budget-smart**
- The outcome she wants people to say: *"Hiring her was the best move we ever made."* This is a goal, not a quote. Never present it as a client testimonial. Use it only as an aspiration line in Rebeca's own voice, e.g. "I want you to look back and say hiring a planner was the best decision you made."

## 4. Faith expression: foundational but quiet

- Occasional scripture at most (about 1 in 8 posts), usually as a closing line, never preachy.
- Content is welcoming to all couples; never assumes the reader shares the faith.

## Voice summary (apply to all copy)
- **Tone**: calm, warm, organized, quietly confident. Talks like a big sister who has a spreadsheet.
- **Point of view**: "I" (Rebeca) to "you" (the couple). Short sentences. One idea per line.
- **Every post relieves one fear**: budget, being stuck fixing problems, decision overwhelm, or not being present.
- **Promise**: "stress-less", never "stress-free". No invented stats, reviews, results or testimonials.
- **Location**: online-based. No Atlanta, Florida or Ocala references.
- **CTAs**: "Comment KIT" or "Comment PLAN"; "link in bio".
